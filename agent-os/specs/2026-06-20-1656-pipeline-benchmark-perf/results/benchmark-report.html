Findings

1 · TTS + ASR are the two universal bottlenecks. Together 55–75% of every run, in every mode. Both scale with the amount of speech in the clip — they dominate regardless of batch vs watch-along.
2 · Batch clears realtime only on sparse content. masha (23% speech) = 0.89×; Fallout (55% speech) = 1.4–1.7×. Speech density is the driver, not a fixed pipeline cost.
3 · The "1.74×" was a cold-start artifact. The first-ever Higgs run per process pays ~240s of model-fetch + MLX Metal-kernel JIT compilation, landing inside the TTS stage wall but not in per-sentence time. Two warm repeats agree at 0.89/0.92 with identical per-sentence rate. Warm is the baseline.
4 · Watch-along is ~2× realtime and carries an 11–13% model-rebuild tax. process_section calls run_job twice per section with fresh model builds — measured 66–80s of pure overhead per 5-min clip. Fully reclaimable with a warm-model loop.
5 · Watch-along re-runs full ASR per section on padded slices. The 8s pad overlaps the next section, so that audio is analyzed twice (masha dubbed-audio 314.7s > 300s source). ASR is the #1 D3 cost on sparse content (34%).
6 · Translate is serial but small (5–15%). ~1.05s/round-trip × N sentences. The concurrent batch candidate already exists (watch-along uses it) — a near-free batch-mode win.

Roadmap — ranked by measured ROI · not yet implemented

  1. Batch translate → batched/concurrent
    effort: near-zero · confidence: high · risk: low
    Serial translate is 12–15% of batch wall; the concurrent openrouter_gemini_flash_batch already exists. → batch RTF −0.10 to −0.18
  2. Watch-along warm-model loop
    effort: medium · confidence: high · risk: medium (32 GB ceiling)
    Build stage models once, process sections in place — reclaims the 11–13% rebuild tax. → watch-along RTF −0.2 to −0.3
  3. Nemotron ASR in watch-along (content-gated)
    effort: medium · confidence: high speed / quality risk
    ASR is 23–34% of D3, Whisper per-section. Nemotron is ~6× faster — but garbles fast cartoon (needs an ear-test gate). → D3 RTF −0.4 to −0.5 (biggest single D3 lever)
  4. TTS per-speaker prefix-KV caching
    effort: high · confidence: medium · highest ceiling
    TTS is 37–52% of every run. Reuse ref+system prefix KV across a speaker's sentences instead of rebuilding per sentence. → −0.1 to −0.2 batch, more in dense D3
  5. Watch-along: stop re-analyzing the pad overlap
    effort: low · confidence: medium · folds into #2
    Each section re-separates/re-ASRs the 8s pad the next section also covers. → RTF −0.05 to −0.15
